About Our Tree Plantation Project

Tree plantation is one of the most important activities for protecting our environment. Trees provide oxygen, reduce pollution, prevent soil erosion, maintain ecological balance and provide shelter for birds and animals.

Through this six-week tree plantation project, we learned about the importance of trees, identified different species, prepared saplings, planted trees, and understood how to protect and maintain them.

Conclusion

The six-week tree plantation project helped us understand the importance of protecting nature. We learned how to plant saplings, water plants, maintain trees and create environmental awareness.
