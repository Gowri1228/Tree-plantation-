🌱 About Our Project

Our Tree Plantation Community Service Project focuses on creating awareness about the importance of trees and environmental protection. Through plantation activities, field visits and community participation, we learned about the importance of greenery, biodiversity, soil conservation, pollution control and climate protection. Our main aim is to encourage people to plant and protect trees for a healthy and sustainable environment.
